VibeCode Labs is a one-person web studio based in Asse, Belgium. I build websites for the local businesses I walk past every day, the bakery on the corner, the café on the square, the barber down the street.

Every site is written in pure HTML and CSS. No heavy frameworks, no page builders, nothing to slow it down. I use AI-assisted development to work faster, and every line is reviewed by a human before it goes live. You should know how your website was made.
