## About

VibeCode Labs is a small web design studio based in Asse, Belgium. We build
fast, mobile-friendly websites for local businesses — bakeries, cafés,
barbershops, and anyone who needs a clean online presence without paying
agency prices.

The studio is run by [Your Name] with the support of a parent/legal guardian,
who co-signs client contracts and handles invoicing. This keeps everything
legal and transparent for both sides.

We focus on:

- Clean, accessible design
- Fast load times (pure HTML/CSS, no bloat)
- Mobile-first layouts
- Privacy-respecting builds (no unnecessary trackers, GDPR-conscious defaults)

---

## Services

### Custom Website Development
A simple, fast, mobile-friendly website for your business. One-page or
multi-page, depending on what you need. Includes contact form, opening hours,
location, and a design that works on every screen.

### Privacy-First Design
We build websites that respect your visitors' privacy. That means no
unnecessary cookies, no third-party trackers, and a clear cookie notice where
required. We are not lawyers — if you need a full legal GDPR audit, we'll point
you to a qualified professional.

### Client Portal (Premium Add-On)
A simple dashboard where you can check your site's uptime, basic visitor stats,
and update your own content. Available as a monthly add-on.

## Our Stack

The landing page is built with pure HTML and CSS — no frameworks, no build
step, no dependencies. The goal is a minimal, high-performance, accessible
site that loads instantly on any device.
